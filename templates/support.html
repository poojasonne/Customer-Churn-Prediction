Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about the churn model

What is the churn probability threshold? +
By default, any probability score ≥ 50.0% is classified as "Likely to Churn" (Result = 1). In production settings, teams often customize this threshold down to 35% to trigger early warning outreach campaigns.
How are predictions recorded in SQLite? +
Every inference made via the `/predict` route is automatically logged into `predictions.db` along with customer tenure, monthly spend, probability %, and timestamp for live historical auditing.
Can I train the model with fresh data? +
Yes! You can add updated records to `data/cleaned2.csv` and run `python ml/train_model.py`. The script fits the pipeline with 200 random forest estimators and exports the new `model.pkl`.
What are the default login credentials? +
For demonstration and evaluation, the portal uses `admin@staywise.com` and password `123456`, or you can use the instant "Guest Access" button on the login screen.
