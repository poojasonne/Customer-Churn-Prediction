About StayWise

StayWise is a machine learning based customer churn prediction platform developed using Python, Flask and Random Forest classification model. The system analyzes customer attributes such as tenure, monthly charges, service usage and contract details to estimate churn probability. Organizations can use this insight to design proactive customer retention strategies and reduce revenue loss.
